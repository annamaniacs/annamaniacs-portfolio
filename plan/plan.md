# Anna Cheng — Portfolio Rebrand, AI Features & Job-Search Strategy

## What it is
A redesigned personal portfolio for Anna Cheng: senior product designer for complex, AI-powered products. It has three AI tools built in, plus a private strategy guide covering the rebrand, the resume rewrite and the job search. The site works on its own as-is, and it can also serve as the blueprint for rebuilding the Framer site.

## Who it's for
- **Recruiters and hiring managers** hiring for Staff/Principal Product Designer, AI Product Designer or Design Director/Head of Design roles. They usually spend under a minute deciding whether to call.
- **Anna**, as the only signed-in user. She uses the private tools to tailor each application and track interest.

## Core features and experience

**Positioning (decided)**
- The brand leads with the name "Anna Cheng". The annamaniacs.com domain stays.
- One main headline covers all three target roles: *"I make complex, high-stakes products clear, including the AI inside them."*
- A "Ways I work" section has three tracks: Hands-on Principal IC, AI Product Design, Design Leadership. Each track links to the case studies that prove it.
- The story leads with AI-first and complex enterprise/fintech work. Retail, healthcare, aviation and public sector appear as proof of range, not as the headline.
- The current site's weak signals are fixed. "600 products" is replaced with impact numbers. Agency-style process steps like "script & moodboard" and "product marketing" are rewritten as a product-design process. Typos are fixed. "29 vs 30 years" is made consistent.

**Public portfolio**
- **Home:** the headline, a short line on what Anna does, the three role tracks, client logos, 4–5 featured case studies, and a clear "Download resume / Contact" call to action.
- **Case studies** follow a recruiter-scannable format: Problem → My role → Key decisions → Outcome (metrics) → What AI changed. The featured set is Bank of America, PNC (21 interviews, 136 fixes, 130+ accessible screens), Highland Homes (first design system + AI-assisted workflow), Phoenix Technologies (leadership) and Meta (safety). GE, Dell and State of Vermont move to a compact "More work" grid. Text comes from the current site and resume. Any metric not in Anna's materials appears as a clearly marked placeholder for her to confirm.
- **About:** a short story focused on the last 15 years. Earlier roles are summarized in one line, which also reduces age-bias risk.
- **Resume page:** the rewritten resume, viewable on the page and downloadable as a PDF.
- **Contact form:** messages are saved to Anna's private area. No email alerts in this phase.

**AI features (Claude Sonnet 5.5, using the Emergent universal key)**
- **"Ask Anna's AI" chat (public):** recruiters ask about her experience, and answers draw only on her resume and case studies. It declines salary, age and personal questions and points people to email instead. All questions are saved so Anna can see what recruiters care about.
- **Job description matcher (public):** a recruiter pastes a job post and sees Anna's matching experience and the most relevant case studies. It deliberately does **not** list gaps publicly.
- **Resume tailoring tool (private):** Anna pastes a job post and gets an honest fit read, including gaps. She also gets a tailored resume summary, reordered bullet points, ATS keywords to add, and a short cover note. Each result can be copied and is saved to her history.

**Private owner area (Anna only, email + password sign-in)**
- The tailoring tool and its history.
- Recruiter chat questions, matcher uses and contact messages, in one inbox.
- **Strategy guide**, a written playbook covering:
  1. Positioning and the brand story.
  2. Resume rewrite rules: three role-specific summary versions, metrics-first bullets, ATS-safe format, a 15-year detail window, and a clear label on contract roles.
  3. Portfolio rules: 4–5 deep case studies beat many shallow ones, plus one dedicated AI case study (gap to fill).
  4. LinkedIn headline and About copy.
  5. A 30/60/90-day job-search plan: target company list by role track, referral outreach scripts, and a weekly application goal.
  6. Interview prep: portfolio walkthrough script, "how you use AI" answer, leadership stories, and seniority questions.
  7. A checklist for moving the new design into Framer.

## Phase 1: what gets built now
- The rebranded public site: home, five full case studies, "More work" grid, about, resume page with PDF download, and contact form.
- "Ask Anna's AI" chat and the job description matcher, live on the public site.
- The private owner area with sign-in, the resume tailoring tool, the questions and messages inbox, and the full strategy guide.
- The rewritten resume content and all new site copy.
- **Not in Phase 1:** editing site content from the owner area (changes go through requests to the builder), email alerts, and analytics.
